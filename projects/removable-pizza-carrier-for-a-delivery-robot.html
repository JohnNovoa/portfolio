Removable Pizza Carrier for a Delivery Robot

Design of a removable, fully mechanical cargo module for a sidewalk delivery robot, sized for a large pizza box.

  • 3D Printing
  • CAD
  • Manufacturing
  • Mechanical parts and Assemblies
  • Product Development

Process & tools

  • Designed an accessory mounted on the robot's existing lid, sized for a large pizza box the standard robot could not carry.
  • Kept the opening mechanism fully mechanical: hinged lid, gas springs and a push-button latch with a cable release.
  • Split the inner frame into a fixed tray and a moving tray, both in laser-cut and bent aluminum sheet metal.
  • Mounted the outer covers, the opening mechanism, the electronics and the inner panels on these two trays.
  • Designed an intermediate part to fix the module to the robot lid.
  • Sealed the joints with O-rings to keep water out.
  • Designed the cosmetic outer covers for SLA printing and PETG inner panels, easy to clean after each delivery.

Impact & results

  • Built 5 units.
  • One unit passed a field test on the robot.
  • The field test showed the first gas springs could not lift the lid on their own. Switched to stronger gas springs, and the lid now opens without help.
  • Next step: more field tests to measure the effect of the added mass and the higher center of mass on the robot.
