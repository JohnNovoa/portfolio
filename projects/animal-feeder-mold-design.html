Animal Feeder Mold Design

Design of injection molds for plastic animal feeders, including reverse engineering of parts, advanced CAD surfacing, and prototyping to validate functionality before manufacturing.

  • CAD
  • CAE
  • Manufacturing
  • Product Development
  • Reverse Engineering

Process & tools

  • Performed reverse engineering of existing feeder designs to generate accurate CAD models.
  • Modeled complex plastic geometries using advanced surfacing tools in SolidWorks.
  • Conducted 3D printing of prototypes to validate size, fit, and usability before mold fabrication.
  • Designed complete injection molds, including cores, cavities, runners, ejectors, and cooling systems.
  • Produced technical drawings with dimensional tolerances, surface finishes, and assembly requirements.
  • Supported during the manufacturing process.

Impact & results

  • Delivered fully functional molds adapted to the client’s production needs.
  • Reduced rework and prototyping costs through early validation with 3D printed models.
