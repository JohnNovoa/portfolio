Packaging Machine Assembly Redesign

Reverse engineering and redesign of a wicketer assembly for a flexible packaging line, fixing premature wear through component redesign and material selection.

  • CAD
  • CAE
  • Manufacturing
  • Product Development
  • Reverse Engineering

Process & Tools

  • Reverse engineered the full assembly in SOLIDWORKS.
  • Found the root causes: excessive wear on the friction parts, a worn drive hub and a weak joining method.
  • Redesigned the friction components and the drive hub to cut wear and improve torque transfer.
  • Adapted the rest of the assembly to the new parts and redesigned the joining method.
  • Selected materials to balance durability, weight and manufacturability.

Impact & Results

  • Fixed the wear and torque transfer problems.
  • The new joining method made maintenance easier.
  • Adopted by the client, cutting downtime in its packaging operation.
