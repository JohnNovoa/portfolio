Snatch Block Lifting Pulleys

Design and validation of a family of snatch blocks (lifting pulleys) for several load ratings, built to the ASME B30.26-2015 standard for lifting equipment in the electrical sector.

  • CAD
  • CAE
  • Manufacturing
  • Product Development
  • Safety Standards
  • SolidWorks

Process & Tools

  • Modeled all pulley components in SOLIDWORKS.
  • Selected materials for the load and safety requirements.
  • Ran structural simulations in ANSYS and SOLIDWORKS Simulation to meet the required design factors.
  • Made manufacturing drawings with tolerances, fits and specifications.
  • Coordinated the manufacturing and assembly of the prototypes.
  • Ran load tests under ASME B30.26-2015.

Impact & Results

  • All prototypes passed the required load tests.
  • The product moved from prototype to series production.
  • Supplied to the electrical sector in Colombia.
