Humanoid Service Robot

Mechanical design of a humanoid robot with robotic arms for object handling, from an industrial design concept to 4 assembled units.

  • Advanced Modeling
  • CAD
  • Manufacturing
  • Product Development

Process & Tools

  • Started from an existing concept and adapted its look and assembly approach to the new robot.
  • Designed the structural and cosmetic parts from scratch in SOLIDWORKS, on a frame built from purchased hardware.
  • Redesigned parts planned for overseas CNC machining and SLA printing so they could be made locally with FDM printing and meet a tight deadline.
  • Split large parts into sections to fit the printers and joined them after printing.
  • Assembled the first units with local parts, then swapped in the overseas parts when they arrived.
  • Supported the team during assembly.

Impact & Results

  • Delivered 4 assembled humanoid robots as an MVP.
  • The robots ran in the field with minor adjustments.
  • Local printing kept the project on schedule.
