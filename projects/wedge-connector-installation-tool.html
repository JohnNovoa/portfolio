Wedge Connector Installation Tool

Design of a mechanical tool for installing wedge connectors on power lines, replacing a tool driven by explosive cartridges.

  • CAD
  • CAE
  • Manufacturing
  • Product Development
  • Reverse Engineering

Process & Tools

  • Reverse engineering and CAD design in SOLIDWORKS.
  • FEA on the cast body to check strength and durability.
  • Functional prototypes in FDM 3D printing.
  • Sand casting patterns and full manufacturing drawings with tolerances.
  • Supported the suppliers during casting and machining.

Impact & Results

  • Removed the recurring cost of explosive cartridges.
  • Crews install connectors with an impact wrench, which is faster and simpler.
  • Adopted by power utilities in Colombia.
