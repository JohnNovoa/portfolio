Mobile Humanoid Robot

Mechanical design of a mobile humanoid service robot, from an industrial design concept to two working units.

  • Advanced Modeling
  • CAD
  • Manufacturing
  • Product Development

Process & Tools

  • Turned an industrial design concept into a complete mechanical design in SOLIDWORKS, with no earlier design to start from.
  • Designed the structure, body, head and outer covers to match the concept.
  • Worked with the electronics engineer to fit sensors, lights and internal electronics into the design.
  • Designed structural supports and internal brackets for CNC machining, bent sheet metal and 3D printing.
  • Integrated robotic arms for object handling, mounted on a horizontal aluminum extrusion.

Impact & Results

  • Built and assembled 2 working units for testing.
  • Completed the development in one month of work.
  • Both units matched the design concept in mechanics, electronics and appearance.
  • Chose a material, process and assembly method for each part that worked for a 2-unit run.
