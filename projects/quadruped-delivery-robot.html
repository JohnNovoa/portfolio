Quadruped Delivery Robot

Mechanical design of the central electronics module for a quadruped delivery robot, built by three designers sharing one PDM vault across two cities.

  • CAD
  • Manufacturing
  • Mechanical parts and Assemblies
  • PDM
  • Product Development

Process & tools

  • Designed the electronics box at the center of the robot. The box joins the commercial quadruped platform, the cargo container and the head.
  • Designed a CNC-machined aluminum base to mount the box on the quadruped.
  • Designed laser-cut and bent sheet metal brackets for the container, plus 3D-printed and sheet metal mounts for the internal electronics.
  • Designed the box's outer covers for SLA printing, the same process used for almost every exterior plastic part on the robot.
  • Agreed the shared mounting points with the other two designers and adjusted each design around them.
  • Set up remote access to SOLIDWORKS PDM through a VPN, so the team worked on one vault from Bogotá and Medellín.
  • Checked teammates' models in PDM to catch fit problems before release.

Impact & results

  • Built 3 working prototypes that met the concept's appearance and function requirements.
  • Completed the development in one month of work.
  • Chose a material, process and assembly method for each part that worked for a 3-unit run.
  • The prototypes appear in the company's public marketing.
